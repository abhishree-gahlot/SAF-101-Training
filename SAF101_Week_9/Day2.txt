Implement different debug levels and showcase differences.

- System.debug() has 5 levels - they tell salesforce how important a message is:
1. System.debug(LoggingLevel.ERROR, 'Something broke. Record it always.');
2. System.debug(LoggingLevel.WARN , 'Something broke. Record it always.');
3. System.debug(LoggingLevel.INFO , 'General progress updates');
4. System.debug(LoggingLevel.DEBUG , 'Checking values during development');
5. System.debug(LoggingLevel.FINE , 'Very detailed tracing, loop-level');
